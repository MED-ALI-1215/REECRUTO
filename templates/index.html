Phase 1 · CV Ingestion

CV Parser

Upload candidate CVs in any format — PDF, DOCX, image, or text. Groq AI extracts structured information automatically and stores candidates in the ChromaDB pool for matching.

Phase 2 · Matching & Decision

Job Matcher

Enter a job description and instantly rank all candidates by fit. Use Groq AI for deep analysis, then Accept to send a personalised AI interview link, or Reject to send a professional rejection email — all in one click.

Open Job Matcher

How it works: Upload CVs via the CV Parser → run Job Matching to find top candidates → click Accept to automatically generate a unique AI interview link and email it to the candidate → candidate completes the interview from their link → results appear automatically in the Dashboard below.
